## A Recent Cybersecurity Incident: ASOS Data Breach (October 2026)
I came across a report about a cybersecurity incident involving ASOS, an online fashion retailer. According to Reuters, an attacker impersonated a trusted contact to obtain an employee's login credentials and then accessed some third party systems. Some customers' personal information, such as names and contact details, was exposed, but ASOS said payment card details and account passwords were not affected.
What interested me most was how impersonation and stolen credentials could lead to a data breach without needing a complicated technical attack. It showed me why verifying identities and securing accounts are important.
